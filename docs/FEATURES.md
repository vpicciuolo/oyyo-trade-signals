# OYYO Trade Signals functionality

## Market research workspace

- Crypto, gold, forex and index research in one workspace.
- Persistent watchlists and reference quotes.
- Structured research records, strategy notes and journal.
- Instrument, horizon, source, freshness and provenance context.
- Supporting and opposing evidence with uncertainty and limitations.
- Coordinated specialist intelligence and contradiction review.

## Alerts and monitoring

- Customer-configured local alert rules.
- Market/session-aware research context.
- Freshness-aware inputs and visible source identity.
- Account and product notifications through the OYYO control plane.

Alerts support monitoring, can be delayed or unavailable and do not execute transactions.

## Paper trading and risk discipline

- Paper tickets for research scenarios.
- Append-only paper transactions.
- Positions derived from the paper ledger.
- Deterministic notional and daily-loss limits.
- Paper kill switch.
- Journal connection for assumptions, outcomes and review.

Paper results do not reproduce every live-market condition, including liquidity, slippage, fees or execution quality.

## Intelligence and models

- OYYO coordination across market, source, evidence and risk responsibilities.
- Device-qualified model execution.
- Model/workflow version attached to outputs.
- Explicit simulated, paper or live-data provenance.
- Traceable source, freshness and limitation information.
- Human-readable synthesis rather than raw model fragments.

## Secure customer account

- Email verification and supported identity providers.
- TOTP multi-factor authentication.
- 72-hour non-converting demo.
- Annual Essential, Pro and Ultimate licences.
- Research add-ons and bundles.
- Device enrollment and revocation.
- Billing, invoices, downloads, support and privacy controls.
- Versioned consent records and account requests.

## Privacy and control

- Research runtime and local records stay with the installed application.
- Account, licence and support functions are separated into the web control plane.
- Local integrations are activated by the customer.
- Third-party connector credentials belong in the operating-system secure store.
- OYYO does not custody customer money, assets, private keys or broker credentials.
