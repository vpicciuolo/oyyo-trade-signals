# Frequently asked questions

## What is Ask OYYO?

Ask OYYO is the private on-device conversation layer in Android 0.8.0. OYYO-SIGNAL-TINY-ALL 2026.11.1 can explain a market or prepare a reviewable analysis, paper-plan or alert proposal from an ordinary-language request. It runs locally and cannot submit a real order from the assistant screen.

## Which model information is shown in the app?

Normal customer screens show the OYYO family name, OYYO version, selected S1–S4 tier and local verification/update status.

## What is OYYO Trade Signals?

A customer-controlled market-intelligence workspace for research, structured observations, alerts, strategy work, paper trading, journaling and risk controls across crypto, gold, forex and indices.

## Why is OYYO different?

OYYO combines the full research loop and makes evidence, counter-evidence, uncertainty, source freshness, limitations, model version and provenance part of every structured output. Coordinated specialists analyze relevant market dimensions before review and synthesis.

## How do the agents work together?

The coordinator scopes the question and assigns relevant specialist analysis. Source, market structure, trend/regime, volatility, news/macro, sentiment and flow responsibilities contribute evidence. The evidence coordinator aligns findings, risk review challenges contradictions and OYYO synthesis produces one readable output. See [Agents and intelligence](AGENTS-AND-INTELLIGENCE.md).

## Is OYYO a broker or investment adviser?

No. It is market-intelligence and decision-support software. It does not provide personal recommendations, custody assets or place trades for customers.

## What does bullish, bearish or neutral mean?

A descriptive market-state label for the selected instrument, horizon, sources and model workflow. It is not a buy, sell or hold instruction.

## Does OYYO show why an output was produced?

Yes. The signal contract includes sources, freshness, supporting evidence, opposing evidence, uncertainty, limitations, model version and provenance.

## Where does my research run?

The installed application owns the research workspace, local records, paper workflows and local model execution. The web control plane handles account, licensing, billing, downloads, notifications, consent records and support.

## Does OYYO store broker or exchange credentials?

No. Customer-enabled local connector credentials belong in the device operating system's secure store and are not stored in the OYYO cloud.

## Does OYYO place trades?

No. Paper tickets simulate user-entered scenarios. OYYO does not offer a cloud order endpoint or real-money execution service.

## Which models does OYYO use?

OYYO uses versioned, device-qualified model workflows. Public model cards and hardware information are available in [OYYO Models](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-models).

## Is there a demo?

Yes. The documented demo lasts 72 hours, is limited to one per account and does not auto-convert.

## Is this repository open source?

No. This is a public customer information repository for a proprietary product. Individual linked artifacts can carry their own licences.

## How do I report a security issue?

Follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Never post vulnerabilities, tokens, credentials or personal data in a public issue.
