# Security policy

## Report privately

Do not open a public GitHub issue for a suspected vulnerability. Use the private contact route at [oyyosignals.trade](https://oyyosignals.trade) or [oyyo.one/contact](https://oyyo.one/contact). Include the affected surface, version, reproducible steps, impact and safely redacted evidence.

Never include live tokens, passwords, private keys, broker/exchange secrets, payment credentials, customer data or another person's information.

## Scope

Reports can cover the OYYO Trade Signals website, account/control-plane behavior, released applications, update/download integrity, authentication, entitlement, local data protection and documented connector boundaries.

## Safe research

- Test only accounts and devices you own or are authorized to test.
- Avoid privacy violations, social engineering, denial of service, spam and destructive testing.
- Do not access, alter or retain another user's data.
- Stop when sensitive data appears and report privately.
- Allow reasonable time for investigation and remediation.

This policy does not create a bug-bounty promise or authorize testing outside OYYO/HRN-controlled systems.
